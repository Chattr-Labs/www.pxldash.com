128 × 64 RGB LED · on your wall

Your wall, lit by what's happening now.

PxlDash drives a small LED panel that hangs like a picture and shows one thing at a time: the flight you're waiting on, the game you're following, the days until a birthday, or the weather.

See the boards

Flights

Your trip, from schedule to gate.

Import a pilot's calendar feed, or track individual flights. PxlDash provides updates pre-flight, during taxi, and during flight to keep the whole house up to date. Between trips it shows what's flying over the house.

Sports

Your teams lead the wall.

Live scores with the game clock, down and distance, and field position. Pick your college and NFL teams; when they're idle the wall shows their next game, their standing, and the rest of the scoreboard.

Countdowns

Count down to the next big day.

Name a date, pick a sprite or two, and the wall counts the days (or the days and hours). Set a time and the last hour ticks down in minutes and seconds. On the day it switches to your own message: a birthday, a holiday, a graduation.

Custom messages

Put your own words on the wall.

Place text, sprites and lines anywhere on the panel, pick a backdrop if you want one, and switch the message on when it's time: a welcome home, a game night, an anniversary.

Animations

Something to watch when nothing's on.

Pick a seasonal animation for the quiet hours. The jack-o'-lantern rests, blinks, flickers, grins and stares, and the panel plays it on its own clock, so it never sits still for long.

Modules

Pick what the wall shows.

Choose the modules that you want to display on the board — set your favorite teams, track flights, count down to a birthday, or just show the weather.
