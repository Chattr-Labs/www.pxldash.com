128 × 64 RGB LED · on your wall

Your wall, lit by what's happening now.

PxlDash drives a small LED panel that hangs like a picture and shows one thing at a time: the flight you're waiting on, the game you're following, or the weather.

See the boards

Flights

Your trip, from schedule to gate.

Import a pilot's calendar feed, or track individual flights. PxlDash provides updates pre-flight, during taxi, and during flight to keep the whole house up to date. Between trips it shows what's flying over the house.

Sports

Your teams lead the wall.

Live scores with the game clock and field position. College football is supported today, with more sports on the way.
